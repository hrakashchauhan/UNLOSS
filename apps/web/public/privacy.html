UNLOSS

Privacy notice

Updated 24 October 2026.

UNLOSS is a parcel-record application operated by its project owner. Contact: hrakashchauhan@gmail.com.

Information used

Accounts contain a name, email address, optional phone number, roles and a password hash when a password is set. Google sign-in supplies a verified Google account identifier, email address and name. UNLOSS does not request access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.

Sellers may enter recipient contact details, delivery addresses, items, tracking references and parcel events. These records support the seller's workspace and parcel workflows. Only enter information you are authorized to provide.

Sessions and hosting

An essential HttpOnly session cookie maintains sign-in, with a seven-day maximum session lifetime. Security controls and server logs process request information for access protection and troubleshooting. Vercel hosts the frontend, Render runs the API, and Supabase stores application records. Google processes Google sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Storage and requests

Account and parcel records remain in the database until removed by the operator. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion; identity verification may be required. This deployment does not yet offer self-service account deletion.

Current service limits

Live SMS verification, anonymous QR mutations, courier-provider updates and payment capture are disabled in this release. Assigned Google-verified accounts can confirm parcel receipt and courier handoff. Optional device locations and manually selected destination pins are visible to the assigned seller, recipient and courier; location records are retained for up to 30 days. Live tracking runs while the page remains open and can be stopped. Loading the map requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which receives the requested map area and network request information. Reward credits and withdrawal receipts are explicitly simulated. Credits remain locked for seven days. No real money is held or transferred and no bank details are required for the simulation. This notice describes the current release; additional data collection requires an updated notice.
